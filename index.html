ABOUT MY WORK

I work on the conditions for
verifiable human–AI systems.

I am an independent researcher and AI systems advisor. I connect research, public archives, protocols, narrative, and systems design to examine what remains after an AI answer: what is remembered, what can be verified, and who remains responsible.

The question guiding my work: how can human intention and machine intelligence act together without losing context, agency, meaning, or evidence?
